Make space for the building to work.

Ground-floor programme is editable geometry. Reservations reduce residential yield, while enclosed service space remains in the conservative floor-area calculation. The PMT is a site reservation outside the floor plates.

All dimensions below are schematic design allowances, not verified minimums. The current official source pages were checked on 19 September 2026. Detailed service-room sizing and PMT installation dimensions could not be verified from the retrieved documents. They must be established for the equipment, building and utility service selected.

Sources and what they establish

City of Vancouver — Building By-law identifies the 2025 VBBL and links its current volumes. Book I, Volume 1, v4 is consolidated to 5 May 2026. Use the applicable service-facility, fire-separation, accessibility and exit provisions, and Book II for plumbing design. The app does not claim a universal VBBL area for any of the listed rooms.

BC Hydro — Electrical service connections states that new multi-occupancy residential buildings require individual metering, subject to the exceptions described there. Provide an electrical / metering strategy, not just a transformer symbol.

BC Hydro — Distribution Technical Standards and Guides lists ES54 F (transformer pads and vaults), the March 2026 F3-06 / F3-07 revisions, ES54 S0-06 (equipment on private property), and secondary service requirements. Detailed downloads require a legal acknowledgement; their dimensions have not been embedded in this app. Review the current applicable standard with BC Hydro before relying on a PMT location.

BC Hydro — Utility clearance guide concerns overhead lines and transformers. Its overhead-transformer distances must not be substituted for pad-mounted-transformer clearances.

The loading allowance is not represented as a verified Parking By-law dimension. Loading classification, number, dimensions and access, water-service specifications, and mail-delivery requirements remain to be checked for the actual project.

Fewer larger upper units are an optional design preference. Their bedroom count remains a packing label until a room-level plan proves furnishing, daylight, accessibility and in-unit travel.
